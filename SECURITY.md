## Reporting a Vulnerability

To report a vulnerability, please follow these steps:

1. **Submit a Report**: Send a detailed description of the vulnerability via a direct message to [@ArkoTest on Twitter/X](https://x.com/arkotest). Include steps to reproduce the issue, the potential impact, and any other relevant information.

2. **Acknowledgment**: You will receive an acknowledgment of your report within 48 hours.

3. **Resolution Timeline**: I aim to provide a resolution or update on the status of the reported vulnerability within 14 days. Critical issues may be addressed more urgently.

4. **Feedback**: If the vulnerability is accepted, you will be informed about the fix and any mitigation steps that should be taken. If declined, you will receive an explanation of why the issue was not deemed critical or actionable.

5. **Disclosure**: I will provide a public disclosure of the vulnerability only after a fix has been released or a reasonable time has passed for users to update to the new version.

Thank you for helping to keep the project secure!
